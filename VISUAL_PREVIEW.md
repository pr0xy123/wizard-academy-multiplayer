# 🎨 Visual Preview: Before & After

## What Changes When You Add the KayKit Models?

### Current Game (Geometric Characters)
```
Your game currently uses simple geometric shapes:
- Cylinders and spheres for the body
- Cones for hats and robes
- Basic colors (red for warrior, purple for mage)
- Static geometric enemies
- Simple cube-based chests
```

### With KayKit Models (Professional 3D)
```
After adding the models, you'll get:
- Detailed low-poly character models
- Smooth skeletal animations
- Professional medieval/fantasy aesthetic
- Consistent art style throughout
- Much more immersive experience
```

- Urgent, energetic motion

## Performance

### Good News!
The KayKit models are specifically designed to be:
- ✅ Low-poly (optimized polygon count)
- ✅ Mobile-friendly
- ✅ Efficient texture usage (single atlas)
- ✅ No performance impact vs geometric shapes

In fact, the models might even perform BETTER than complex geometric setups because they're professionally optimized!

## File Size

### Character Models:

### Environment (Optional):
